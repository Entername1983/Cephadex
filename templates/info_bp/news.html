Updates:


22/04/2023

Biggest update in a long time!
New features
  • Your role (student, teacher...) is used to customize the data you see as well as the user interface.
  • Answer keys are now available for tests/worksheets
  • Improved group functionality, add public decks directly to a group or share your own.
  • You will now be notified by email when a deck is created, very handy if you have very large files to process
    • Design
  • Revamped a lot of the pages with a dated UI/graphics
  • Clicking on a deck will now take you to the deck manager for that deck, where you can find files, assignments and of course cards in that deck
  • You can also see child and parent decks in the deck manager, this is a great feature for organizing all your content.
  • We have a few more re-designs coming out at the end of this week. Stay tuned!
    • Bug fixes
  • After some security improvements some bugs popped up in the test creation feature. Those have now been fixed.
  • Fixed a bug with fill in the blank questions
  • Fixed a bug with account settings sometimes not saving properly
  • We made some big improvements on how audio files get handled.
  • There was an issue where some youtube videos were not processable - this is either the owner of the video has made the video captions private, or in rare cases because captions are not availble (more likely in languages other than English). We have implemented messaging for when that is the case.
    • Account
  • We have changed the subscription plans to have 3 tiers to better suit the needs of our users. Basic - Standard - Premium. Enterprise solutions remain available.
  • We implemented a credit system to make usage more transparent. 1 credit corresponds to roughly between 1 and 4 pages of content. You can see how many credits each content creation process would take before doing so.

  • Groups are now fully functional. You can now add and remove members from groups. You can also add and remove decks from groups.
