Kevin - Founder

I'm Kevin, the guy behind the computer behind Cephadex. With a decade-long journey in education, starting out teaching English as a second language, before branching into Economics and Business at both A-Level and International Baccalaureate (IB). These years in education were incredibly rewarding, but following the global pandemic, I felt an urge to navigate a fresh path.

Thus, I found myself swapping places and embracing the role of a student again. I was particularly captivated by CS-50 on EdX, a course whose design left an indelible mark on me. The fusion of elegant course architecture and profound content sparked an idea - creating my own edtech webapp.

In a fortunate coincidence, this inspiration struck just as Artificial Intelligence began to dominate headlines and conversations. Recognizing the vast potential of AI, I resolved to integrate these emerging tools into the realm of education, believing it to be a perfect fit for my passion and experience.

My mission with Cephadex is to develop a platform that truly simplifies and enriches the lives of educators, students, and parents alike. I think I am on that path by creating a platform that is easy to use, yet powerful enough to help you create engaging and effective study materials.

I welcome and deeply appreciate any feedback or suggestions that can help me accomplish this ambitious goal.
