Game Lobby

This is our version of apples to apples. The creator of the game loads a deck and invites players to the game. Each round players will be presented with a prompt and have to come up with their own answer, after which the correct answer, as well as all the player submitted answers are revealed. At this point each player will need to vote on what they believe is the correct answer. Points are awarded for correct answers (2 points) and for votes on your answer (1 point). The player with the most points at the end of the game wins.

The answers expected depend on the type of deck loaded and the prompt. For example a term might be presented to the players and they would have to write the best definition possible. If the prompt is a question they would have to come up with the most convincing answer.
