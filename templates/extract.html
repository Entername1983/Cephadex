Main options explained:
  • Definitions: Our basic type of card, we will look for key terms in the content submitted and provide you with a definition for each.
  • Multiple Choice Questions: One of our favourites, we look for the key ideas and provide you with multiple choice questions to test your understanding.
  • Translate: Are you learning a new language? This option will look for key terms and provide you with a translation. Make sure to choose the language in which you want your content translated
  • Formulas: For the quantitavely inclined. This will look for any formula associated with the content and created a card with said formula as well as an explanation.
  • Fill in the blanks: A great one for language learners, the name says it all. We will select some key passages from the content and omit one or more words.
  • Theories: More academically oriented, this option identifies key theories and concepts identified in the passage and creates a card with an explanation.
  • Rhyme: Perhaps the most memorable way to learn. We identify key terms in the text and create a short descriptive poem to ensure that you'll never forget it.
  • Comprehension: Great for testing yourelf or students. We create a bunch of questions that test your overall comprehension of the material.
  • People: Are you passionate about biographical information? This one is for you. We identitify people named in the passage submitted and provide a short biography.
  • Vocabulary builder: Another great one for language learners, like definitions but this time we are looking at every single word in the passage.
  • Transcribe: Use this to transcribe an audio file or in combination with our language option to translate entire documents.
  • Summarize: Have big documents or audio files that need to be summarized? Use this option. Note: not yet implemented
  • Turn to notes: Do you have an audio recording of a lecture? Did you take notes but need them reorganized? This option is for you. Note: not yet implemented
  • Save text: If you check this box we will save the text you submitted in source docs. If you select the transcribe option this will automatically be checked. You can delete these files at any time.
Advanced options explained:
    For advanced users who want to refine their results.
  • Minimum items per page: Sets a minimum amount of cards per page submitted.
  • Minimum items per page: Sets a maximum amount of cards per page submitted.
  • Subject: Are you trying to focus on a particular subject? Choose this option to have the results be more specialized.
  • Content length:This will affect the amount of detail in the card produced. For example you could choose whether you want brief definitions or lengthy ones.
  • Output language:Perhaps you want a term in English but the definition to be in another language? In that case you can use this option. Note: it is not necessary to use this option if you are looking for regular translations
  • Generate images:We will generate an image for each card created. Note: this can significantly affect the time it takes to generate results.
